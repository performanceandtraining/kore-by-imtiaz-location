          Sources and notes

          How to read this guide

          • Outside-area figures use Kore’s mapped project approach at the same dated 11:00 pm departure. Opening hours are separate: HealthHub Silicon Oasis lists 8 am–10 pm, so its 11 pm journey is not an opening-hours claim.
          • Markers identify named-site Google vehicle-arrival selections. Confirm visitor gates, parking and terminal entry before travelling. DXB refers to Terminal 3’s vehicle approach.
          • Within labels describe the plotted point relative to the approximate area outline. They do not establish a surveyed campus boundary. FirstPoint’s mapped arrival is outside this outline; Aquila and Aster are within it.
          • The Aquila KHDA map is offset from the named Google campus. The named Google arrival is retained. School choices are a shortlist, without a nearest ranking.
          • Fees are published undiscounted annual tuition for 2026–27; required supplies and optional services cost extra. Overall inspection ratings are dated 2023–24. ECC means early childhood centre, FS means Foundation Stage, and IB means International Baccalaureate.
          • Rental figures and a matching apartment resale series were unavailable in the inspected sources. No growth, yield or exit-period claim is made.
          • The online basemap needs internet. Local shapes, text and directions remain available if tiles fail. No measured driving-route lines are supplied.
